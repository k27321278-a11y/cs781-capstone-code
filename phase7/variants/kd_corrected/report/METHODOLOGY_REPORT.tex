\noindent\textbf{Status.} Post-run methodology reconstruction grounded in archived source and reports. This document is not an executed historical notebook.\par
\section*{Research question}
Compress the full retrieval model's ranking signal into a smaller student. The GPU report names full as teacher and lists about 67.8 million trainable student parameters.
\section*{Architecture and mechanism}
The full model supplies teacher targets. A lower-capacity student learns to rank the same candidate pools; its outputs are exported with the self-match rule enforced.
\section*{Measured result}

Corrected local dev micro-F1@7 was 0.3969 and Codabench was 0.4034. The hidden result is close to the full model but does not exceed it.
\section*{Limitations and next check}
Teacher-target quality and distillation temperature/loss affect this comparison; the reported score alone cannot isolate them. Local, pilot, and hidden metrics are deliberately kept distinct. A single scored run does not establish a causal architecture effect or statistical significance.

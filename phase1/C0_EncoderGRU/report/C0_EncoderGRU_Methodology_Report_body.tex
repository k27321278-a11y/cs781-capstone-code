\section{C prompt encoder + GRU generator}
\subsection{Architecture} Ten bidirectional Transformer blocks encode the prompt. A four-layer GRU decoder, initialized from mean prompt memory, generates in FP32 and cross-attends to encoded context; SwiGLU fusion and tied embeddings produce token logits.
\subsection{Evidence} 0.8237 (historical Codabench screenshot). Original source and logs are indexed alongside this section; the illustrated notebook was reconstructed after training.

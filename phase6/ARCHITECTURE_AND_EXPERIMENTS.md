# Phase 6: legal summarization design progression

The task maps a long judgment to a shorter summary. A limited encoder context makes source selection part of the architecture: the model only generates from evidence it can see. We therefore kept passage selection document-only and preserved first/last coverage, rather than using a reference summary to choose test passages. Training minimizes target-token negative log likelihood, `L = -sum_t log p(y_t | y_<t, x)`, but that loss is not the same as the scored Codabench BERTScore F1. Saved reports and submission scores are kept separate throughout the variant packages.

The first group varies how a Phase 1 legal model is adapted. [B1 passage fusion](variants/b1_fusion/README.md) represents several passages, composes their evidence, and generates autoregressively; its scored BERTScore F1 was 0.7674. [C1 Houlsby](variants/c1_houlsby/README.md) inserts small bottleneck residual adapters into the inherited C1 path and scored 0.7700. [D222 prefix](variants/d222_prefix/README.md) instead learns attention-conditioning prefixes and scored 0.7710. These are different adaptation mechanisms as well as different parent models, so their score differences cannot be assigned to adapters alone.

We next tested capacity and stopping behavior. [B1 AdaLoRA](variants/b1_adalora/README.md) reallocates the low-rank update budget during training; it scored 0.7656. [C1 continuation](variants/c1_continue/README.md) resumes the same C1 architecture from its own checkpoint and scored 0.7671, lower than its earlier export. Continuation is an optimization experiment, not another architecture. Similarly, the [B1 300-word](variants/b1_fusion_length/README.md) and [C1 400-word](variants/c1_houlsby_length/README.md) entries reuse model weights but change the exported generation length. Their caps were chosen using held-out validation subsets and later checked on separate confirmation cases; Codabench scores were 0.7783 and 0.7750. The B1 increase is consistent with a length mismatch between unconstrained generations and reference style, but the current evidence does not establish that shorter is always better.

Finally, [LegalSpan LoRA](variants/legalspan_lora/README.md) changes the parent representation to the Phase 1 LegalSpan lineage and adapts selected projections with low-rank modules. Its saved report chose epoch 4 by validation negative NLL; the scored export reached 0.7847, the best of these submitted Phase 6 variants. The comparison suggests that source representation and passage coverage mattered, while parent-model, training, and decoding changes prevent a single-variable causal claim. BERTScore also cannot by itself certify legal fact fidelity or citation correctness.

The [submission map](SUBMITTED_VARIANTS.md) lists all eight distinct scored exports, their visible IDs, and the screenshot. Each linked package contains original code, source hashes, evidence, a later explanatory notebook, and PDF/LaTeX methodology notes.
